Who We Are

For collectors who want their favorite machines in the room.

We started Prism Frames with a simple idea: automotive and motorsport fandom deserves better than generic decor. Your favorite car, race bike, team, or poster should feel intentional inside the room.

Our process is collaborative from the first message to the final piece. Whether you want a 3D car figure, an F1 display, a MotoGP themed piece, or a premium framed poster, we shape it around your taste and space.

Studio Principle

We do not just sell decor. We create display pieces with presence, detail, and fan energy.
